About Alex

  • Road trip enthusiast who regularly drives long distances to capture landscapes
  • Tech-savvy, uses both laptop and mobile but often relies on her phone while travelling
  • Prefers simple, fast digital tools that don't waste her time

Goals

  • Plan journeys efficiently
  • Save money by choosing the best-priced station
  • Reduce hassle during travel so she can focus on photography

Needs

  • Wants to save time by quickly finding stations that match her criteria (e.g., has a car wash or food)
  • Wants to make informed choices by comparing fuel prices across stations
  • Wants to avoid frustration by navigating a mobile-friendly site without pinching and zooming
  • Wants to travel confidently knowing she has access to all the services she needs along the way

Pain Points

  • Current filtering is confusing and unintuitive
  • Cannot view or compare fuel prices directly on the map
  • Has to visit multiple websites or call stations to check prices.

Behaviours / Preferences

  • Uses Google Maps and apps like CamperMate when planning trips
  • Looks for speed and clarity over fancy features
  • Will consider competitors if they provide easier tools

Design Implications

  • Prioritise clarity in navigation and filtering
  • Show fuel prices directly on map pins and lists
  • Ensure responsive, mobile-first design
  • Highlight travel-friendly features and services
